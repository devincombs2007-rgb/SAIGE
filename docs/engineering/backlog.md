## Post-Showcase-04 correction and advancement program (owner directive, 2026-11-26)

Showcase 04 is finished and is never remade; the next generation of the engine is what changes. Defects and their evidence: `docs/showcase/04-defect-register.md`. Order: P0 correctness, P1 foundational realism, P2 advanced fidelity, P3 scalability. Nothing here replaces the sections below; each item is also tracked in them.

* **P0 critical correctness.** Done: interior/facade coplanar surfaces, dropped walls, scenery overlapping the real road, parking-bay furniture, camera in foliage/awnings, tree leaves through buildings, unusable interiors, with the district validator (`district_validate`) and the visual regression (`alive_visual`, mutants caught). Next: lane graph and crosswalk validation, curb transitions, stair headroom and railings, interior reachability, wrong-winding detection, the unidentified black props, mesh-level overlap checks, the authored building kit (repetition and proportions), a frame-by-frame sweep of the master for defects nobody listed.
* **P1 foundational realism.** Local illumination first (point and spot lights on the RHI compute/indirect program: clustered assignment, then headlights, brake lights, emergency lights, streetlights, interiors; emissive is not light), stable shadow cascades (diagnostic overlay and a consecutive-frame stability test first), glass and reflections (IBL, alpha-blended glass), exposure continuity, contact shadows. Characters: measure foot slide and contact on the film's clips, wire foot placement, stepping turns, stairs, ragdoll recovery. Vehicles: measure wheel/steering/suspension sync against the physics body, tire contact, weight transfer, vehicle-vehicle collisions. Runtime audio, physical interactions. Cameras: collision, obstruction avoidance (`view_obstructed` is the start; make it an engine capability), chase cameras, framing checks.
* **P2 advanced fidelity.** Professional assets (licensed, LOD, instancing, streaming), global illumination and IBL, vegetation with wind, weather, water (boats, wakes, foam), destruction, advanced materials, volumetric smoke and atmosphere.
* **P3 scalability.** The RHI compute/indirect/instancing/culling program continues (`docs/rendering/`); streaming, memory budgets, geometry and texture LOD, population density. Software Vulkan results prove correctness; **only a physical GPU run is performance evidence**.
* **Quality infrastructure (alongside P0).** Permanent diagnostic render modes (wireframe, collision, navigation, normals, material ids, depth, cascade boundaries, caster visibility, overlap, foot and wheel contacts, vehicle paths, NPC targets, light contributions) and visual regression (consecutive-frame stability, shadow stability, geometry visibility, camera and character movement, simulation-to-render sync, lighting transitions). Started: `--disable`, `--check-camera`, `SAIGE_ALIVE_*` switches, the visual cases.
* **Incident realism (for Gemini's world simulation, Build 010 stays an experimental baseline and is not integrated blindly).** Incidents resolved by completed activities, not elapsed time: responders exiting, assessment, treatment, road closure and diversion, fire suppression, tow dispatch and physical recovery.
